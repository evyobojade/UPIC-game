Quick guide

How to play

Everything you need to get your campus quest started.

One word connects the pictures

Look at all four picture clues and work out the word they have in common. Tap letters on the randomized keyboard to fill each slot. Tap a filled slot to remove its letter; the puzzle checks your answer once all the slots are filled.

Your toolbar

Use hint: reveals a random letter. You can hold up to three hints. Visit buildings around the map to earn hints, even before their levels are unlocked. Each building can award one exploration hint; if your hint pouch is full, return after spending one. Swap pictures: shuffles the clues and brings in a fresh set of related images. Tap a filled answer slot to remove an individual letter.

Unlock the map

Solve each puzzle to reveal that building on the map. Levels unlock in order; select an unlocked building to travel there, play again, or learn more about it.

Level 1

The first puzzle's answer is a seven-letter word connected to books and the library. Can you find it using the pictures?
